Coordinator Quick Guide

Welcome to the Familia'26 Coordinator Command Center. Here is how to control the live retreat prayer session:

🎯
1. Trigger Remote Spin
Click "TRIGGER REMOTE SPIN" to immediately trigger the wheel on all connected projector screens and laptops in real-time.
🔄
2. Managing Rounds
When all families are prayed for, the main button turns green to "START ROUND X NOW". You can also click "⏩ Next Round" or use the + / − buttons anytime.
👥
3. Add, Edit & Filter Families
Use Bulk Edit to paste lists from Google Sheets or add families one-by-one. Mark prayed or restore families back to the wheel as needed.
📥
4. Export & Logs
Export prayer log records to CSV or copy text summary to share with ministry leaders.
